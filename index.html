Our Story

Craftsmanship Over Shortcuts

Angel Nails was built on one belief: you shouldn't have to choose between beautiful nails and healthy nails. Every service at our Roxy Square studio is performed by hand — no electric drills on natural nails, no harsh acrylics unless you want them, and sterilised tools for every single client.

Our nail artists train in precision gel work and hand-painted art because the details matter. A perfectly aligned French tip, a gradient that fades just right, rhinestone placement that stays put for weeks — that's what separates a nail salon from a nail experience.
