Gel Polish & Longevity

How long does gel polish last at Angel Nails?

With proper curing and quality gel systems, our gel polish typically lasts 3–4 weeks without chipping or lifting. We use calibrated LED lamps and professional-grade products — under-cured gel is the most common reason for early peeling at other salons, and it's something we've eliminated from our process entirely.

Why does gel polish peel or lift at some salons but not here?

Three common causes: improper nail prep, low-quality gel, and under-curing. We address all three. Our manicurists prep the nail plate by hand to the correct texture (without over-filing), use professional gel systems designed for adhesion, and cure every layer fully under LED. The result is a bond that holds.

Is your gel polish safe? Will it damage my nails?

Our gel products are free from the most harmful chemicals (formaldehyde, toluene, DBP). More importantly, the way we apply and remove gel matters most. We never scrape gel off with force — proper soak-off technique means your natural nail stays intact between sets.

Nail Health & Technique

Do you use electric drills on natural nails?

No. We use hand tools for all cuticle work and natural-nail preparation. Electric drills are only used when technically necessary — for hard-gel or acrylic removal — and always at controlled, low speed. This protects your nail plate from thinning and heat damage that's common at high-volume salons.

My nails feel thin and weak after visiting other salons. Can you help?

This usually results from aggressive filing or forced gel removal. We can assess your nail condition and recommend a recovery plan — whether that's a period of nail masks and strengthening treatments, a gentle overlay for protection while your nails grow out, or simply a proper manicure with no-damage techniques.

Do gel extensions damage natural nails?

Not when applied and removed correctly. Our team understands nail anatomy, apex placement, and stress points, so extensions sit naturally and don't create leverage that snaps at the free edge. Proper soak-off removal — not prying — is equally important, and we never skip that step.

Are your tools sterilised between clients?

Yes. All metal tools are sterilised between every client. Files and buffers are single-use or sanitised per protocol. Workstations are wiped clean before each appointment. This is a non-negotiable part of our process — not an optional extra.

Nail Art & Design

Can you do complex nail art designs?

Complex art is one of our strongest capabilities. Florals, character designs, gradients, 3D elements, and intricate line-art are all hand-painted freehand by our artists — not printed from a machine or stamped from a plate. Send your design reference via WhatsApp and we'll confirm pricing and timing before your appointment.

How do I share my design idea?

Screenshot or save the design you like and send it to us on WhatsApp (9636 3537). Include the number of nails you'd like art on, and we'll reply with a clear price quote before you book.

What's the difference between hand-painted art and stamped/printed nails?

Stamped or printed designs are applied from pre-made templates — they're fast and cheap, but limited in detail, colour depth, and customisation. Hand-painted art is built layer by layer with fine brushes, allowing true colour blending, gradient control, and unique one-off designs that can't be replicated from a plate.

Pricing & Booking

Why is Angel Nails more expensive than some salons nearby?

Our pricing reflects the time, technique, and products involved. We use professional-grade gel systems, hand tools instead of high-speed drills, sterilised instruments for every client, and hand-painted nail art rather than stamps or stickers. The result is longer-lasting, healthier nails — which means fewer return visits and less damage over time. Gram for gram, you're often paying less in the long run.
