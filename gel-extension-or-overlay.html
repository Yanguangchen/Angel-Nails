Gel extension or overlay gel

Angel Nails lists both services at 50 E Coast Rd, Roxy Square, #02-21. They solve different problems. A gel extension adds length. Overlay gel is priced per piece and is the smaller add-on when a nail needs structure without a full set of new length.

The published prices

  • Gel extension: $35 to $48.
  • Overlay gel: $5 to $15 per piece.

Those ranges are on the service menu. The number inside each range depends on the work in front of the artist. Ask which end applies before the appointment starts.

When an extension is the job

Book a gel extension when you want length you do not already have. The studio’s position, also on the FAQ, is that extensions do not damage natural nails when they are applied and removed correctly: apex placement, attention to stress points, and soak-off rather than prying. Electric drills are reserved for hard-gel or acrylic removal, and only at a controlled low speed.

When overlay is enough

Overlay gel is listed separately, by the piece. It is the option when one or a few nails need reinforcement and you are not asking for a full extension set. If most of the set needs length, the extension line is the clearer booking. If you are repairing a break on nails that are already the length you want, say that when you message so the chair is planned as overlay work.

What to say when you book

  • Whether you want added length or support on the length you have.
  • How many nails are broken, short, or uneven.
  • Whether hard gel or acrylic is already on the nail and needs soak-off first.

Soak-off hard gel is its own menu line. Removal and a new set are not the same appointment length. Name both if you need both.
