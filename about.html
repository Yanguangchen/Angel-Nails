About Angel Nails

Technical Skill. Honest Care. Beautiful Results.

We're not the cheapest salon on East Coast Road — and that's by design. Over a decade of focused training in gel systems, hand-painted art, and natural-nail health is what sets us apart from every other option in Roxy Square and Marine Parade.

Why We Exist

The East Coast corridor has no shortage of nail salons. Walk through Roxy Square or along Marine Parade and you'll find express shops offering fast colour changes at rock-bottom prices. Many salons focus on speed. Angel Nails focuses on lasting results, careful technique, and protecting your natural nails — never filing too aggressively, skipping proper gel curing, or using products that weaken the natural nail over time.

Angel Nails was founded to be the alternative: a studio where speed never comes at the expense of technique. Where every manicurist is trained to protect your natural nail first and deliver stunning results second. Where you leave knowing your nails are stronger than when you walked in.

What Makes Us Different

Hand-Tool-First Approach

Most salons in the area default to electric drills for speed. We use hand tools for cuticle work and natural-nail preparation — slower, but dramatically safer. Drills come out only for hard-gel or acrylic removal when technically required, and always at controlled, low speed.

Proper Gel Systems & Curing

We work with professional-grade gel polish and builder gels that are fully cured under calibrated LED lamps. This matters because under-cured gel is the leading cause of peeling, lifting, and allergic reactions — problems you'll rarely encounter here.

Hand-Painted Nail Art

Our artists paint freehand. Florals, line-art, gradients, character designs — they're done brush-by-brush, not printed from a machine or stamped from a plate. The precision shows, and the result is truly one-of-a-kind.

Extension & Sculpture Expertise

Gel extensions, acrylic sculpting, and overlay work require real structural knowledge. Our team understands nail anatomy, apex placement, and stress points — which means extensions that look natural, feel balanced, and don't snap at the first bump.

Sterilisation, Every Time

Metal tools are sterilised between every client. Files and buffers are single-use or sanitised per protocol. Our workstations are wiped clean before you sit down. It sounds basic, but walk into most express salons and observe how rarely this actually happens.

Transparent Pricing

We quote before we start — and the price you're quoted is the price you pay. No hidden charges for nail art "complexity" added after the fact. Bring your design reference, and we'll confirm the cost upfront.

The Roxy Square & Marine Parade Difference

Clients in this neighbourhood have high standards — and plenty of options. We've earned our place by being the salon people recommend when someone asks "where do I go for nails that actually last?" or "who can do complex art without destroying my nails?"

That reputation isn't built on marketing. It's built on a decade of consistent results: gel that lasts 3–4 weeks without lifting, extensions that stay put, and natural nails that grow stronger under our care rather than thinner.

Beyond Nails

We've expanded carefully into complementary services — eyelash extensions, facial treatments, IPL hair removal, and cosmetic nano tattooing — applying the same philosophy: proper training, quality products, honest pricing. Every new service meets the same standard we set for nails.
