How long gel polish lasts in Singapore humidity

Angel Nails is at 50 East Coast Road, Roxy Square, #02-21. The FAQ answer on gel wear is specific: with proper curing and a quality gel system, gel polish typically lasts 3–4 weeks without chipping or lifting. Under-cured gel is called out as the usual reason polish peels early somewhere else. Humidity is the reason that curing step matters. It is not a reason the salon publishes a shorter guarantee.

Which booking matches a gel manicure

The published prices on the contact page, in SGD, are the ones to use when you message:

  • Express gel polish manicure: $38
  • Full classic manicure with gel polish: $45
  • Express gelish pedicure: $38
  • Gelish pedicure: $55
  • French gel polish, as an add-on: $15
  • Soak-off gelish: $15–$25

A plain express manicure is $20 and a classic manicure is $28. Those are not gel services. If you want the 3–4 week wear the FAQ describes, book a gel line, not the regular polish line.

When to book a soak-off instead of a new set

Lifting at the cuticle, a grown-out smile line, or gel that has been on past the month you planned are reasons to book soak-off rather than paint over the old layer. Soak-off gelish is $15–$25. Soak-off for hard gel is listed separately at $15–$45, because extensions and overlays are a different material. The FAQ is clear that removal is a soak, not prying. Gel extensions themselves are $35–$48, and overlay gel is $5–$15 per piece.

What helps the weeks you do get

The salon’s own explanation is calibrated LED curing and professional-grade gel, plus removal that does not lever the nail. Gloves for long wet work, and not using the nail as a tool, are the practical half of that. If a set lifts in the first week, tell the studio rather than peeling it off at home. Peeling takes the surface of the nail with it.
